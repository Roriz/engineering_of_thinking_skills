# Company knowledge pack

Use a small, linked set of Markdown documents. These are suggested boundaries, not mandatory empty files. Merge or omit thin/irrelevant topics. Retain known facts and consequential gaps even in a partial draft; do not force the caller through every topic before delivering value.

## Index and resumable state

`index.md` states the company, intended use/audience, language, preparation date, draft/review state, and links to the documents created. Keep the source inventory here or in a separate source register if it becomes large. Inventory entries include source ID, title/type, location, date/version when known, review coverage, and any material usage restriction supplied by the caller.

Maintain a concise gap list with the unresolved fact/decision, why it matters, conflicting source IDs if any, the smallest useful next question or evidence check, and whether it is blocking or deferred. Record the current interview round, relevant answer summaries, and next questions so a later session can resume without asking settled questions again. Do not mark the pack as approved without an actual review.

## Topic documents

| Suggested file | Purpose and essential distinctions |
| --- | --- |
| `company-and-brand.md` | Canonical names and entity relationships; what the company does; mission/voice when relevant; location and service area; established facts versus proposed identity or aspirations. |
| `offerings.md` | Current products/services, capabilities, exclusions, suitability, delivery, availability, purchase channels, and sourced commercial terms; distinguish roadmap and retired versions. |
| `customers-and-buying-journey.md` | Actual customer segments and examples, users/buyers/approvers, triggers, tasks, objections, decision criteria, and alternatives; separate history, interview recollection, and proposed ICP. |
| `market-and-industry.md` | Category vocabulary, scope and geography, ecosystem, trends, industry-specific constraints, and sourced external observations; no invented market size or authoritative legal interpretation. |
| `positioning-and-alternatives.md` | Current positioning, direct and indirect alternatives, comparison criteria, evidence-backed strengths and limitations, and explicitly proposed positioning decisions. |
| `evidence-and-claims.md` | Reviewable claim register and case evidence; metrics, methods, scope, qualifiers, contradictory evidence, and unresolved substantiation/disclosure questions. |
| `audience-questions.md` | Questions by audience, intent, and decision stage; distinguish observed customer wording from synthesized hypotheses; link each answerable question to facts/evidence and record the gaps. |

Do not duplicate canonical pricing in five places. Link to the offering fact and retain its date, currency, unit, conditions, and disclosure context. Do not merge a parent company and product into one entity or treat aspirational buyers as actual clients.

## Claim register

Use stable IDs when the pack needs cross-document traceability. A useful compact shape is:

| ID | Statement | Basis/status | Source and locator | Scope/date and qualifications | Disclosure context / unresolved question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

The evidence rules define status semantics. Register material claims, disputed facts, and consequential unknowns rather than making a database row for every incidental phrase. Link detailed documents to the relevant claim entry or directly to a source. A note such as “user-reported in interview round 2” identifies a source; it is not independent verification.

## Derived assets and downstream handoff

Create a fact sheet, comparison-criteria brief, case-study outline, or answer brief only when it serves the requested use and has enough evidence. Include evidence links, unknowns, and use restrictions. Avoid generating collateral merely to increase output volume.

A downstream GEO audit needs the actual audience/market, product boundaries, target decisions, brand relationships, important claims, and evidence gaps. Supply those links and summarize unsettled assumptions. The pack is context, not proof that the site meets an aspect or that a model recommends the company. A content task can use the question map and claim register, but publication still requires an explicit publishing instruction and accurate disclosure handling.
