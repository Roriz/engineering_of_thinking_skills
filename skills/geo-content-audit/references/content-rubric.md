# Shared content rubric

Apply aspects according to the item's role and the discovery pack. A checklist feature is not a ranking factor. For each relevant aspect record status, content evidence, company-reference evidence when applicable, and the reason.

| Aspect | What to assess | Important boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audience and intent | Which documented buyer/reader question or task is served; whether the content delivers what its hook/title promises. | A useful educational post need not sell. Synthesized questions are not measured demand or model fan-outs. |
| Company and entity alignment | Correct company/product names, current capabilities, exclusions, geography, customer status, and positioning. | Distinguish historical facts, current facts, roadmap, proposals and aspirations. Preserve unresolved conflicts with the pack. |
| Answer value | A meaningful explanation, useful example, criteria, trade-off, or practical insight that advances the intended task. | Do not require exhaustive coverage, FAQs, numerical claims, or competitor names in every item. |
| Claim integrity | Whether factual, quantitative, comparative, outcome, and market assertions are traceable and scoped. | Interview/company claims are not independent proof. A selected case is not a typical result; an association is not causality. |
| Context and interpretability | Clear referents, applicable audience/market, qualifications, language and scope; coherence between title, body, media and caption. | Readability does not establish retrieval or technical indexability. A short excerpt may legitimately link to detail. |
| Evidence and source fit | Whether inspected sources support the relevant claims, with methods/date/denominator where material; original information versus unsupported assertions. | A logo, citation count, badge or testimonial is not proof by itself. Self-published content does not become independent corroboration. |
| Decision support | Relevant comparison criteria, limitations, suitability, and next steps for this item's job. | Not every post needs a CTA or purchase intent. Do not invent alternative rankings or pricing. |
| Disclosure and attribution | Identified source/author relationships, accurate quotations, and known constraints on customer/case material. | Internal possession is not public permission. Unknown permission calls for verification, not a claim that a violation occurred. |
| Format and connected evidence | Whether the format makes the useful information understandable and supports a sensible path to related evidence. | Assess blog and social mechanics separately. Missing inspection evidence is Unknown, not a defect. |

## Handling claims

For a material claim, identify the exact text, relevant discovery source/claim ID, and any inspected substantiation. Preserve units, population, geography, time window, comparison basis, typicality, exclusions, and relevant currency. An owner-reported correction can establish a reported discrepancy; do not call it independent verification. Resolve important external facts with actual sources when within scope; otherwise state the verification needed.

When recommending a sample edit, use existing supported facts. Do not invent a statistic, customer quote, qualification, award, author credential, market definition, or regulatory conclusion to make the content sound stronger. If a result is unsubstantiated, recommend removing or qualifying it, or obtaining the actual evidence; do not fabricate methodology.

## What this audit cannot measure

Without response data, model visibility, mention frequency/order, retrieved-source presence, and displayed citation share are Unknown. A single manual answer is an example, not a stable benchmark. Content quality and source accessibility do not establish that a specific assistant uses the material. Likes, comments, impressions and follower counts, if supplied, describe their own platform metrics and sample; they are not citation measurements or proof of business impact.

Do not claim specific word counts, hashtag patterns, link placement, FAQ markup, publishing cadence, competitor mentions, or English translation guarantee GEO lift. If a current platform/provider rule becomes material, verify it using current primary documentation rather than presenting remembered behavior as fact. Keep any outcome experiment bounded and separate from finishing the editorial corrections.
