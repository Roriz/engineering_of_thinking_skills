# Content audit report

Scale the report to the batch. A single short post may need only a compact assessment and a few recommendations; do not inflate it into a campaign strategy.

## Scope and verdict

Identify the discovery pack/version, reviewed documents and claim IDs, supplied items and exact versions, channel/format, purpose, audience and market. Record limits such as draft-only inspection or missing carousel slides. Summarize the strongest content and most consequential supported gaps. Distinguish discovery context from observed content evidence.

## Priorities

Show T0–T3; state when a tier has no supported action. Put checks outside confirmed defect tiers. Shared issues get one action with affected item IDs.

| Tier / finding | Item and exact location | Concrete action | Why this priority | Completion check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Use qualitative effort only when context supports it. Avoid fabricated scores, budgets, timelines or impact percentages.

## Per-item assessment

Give the item purpose and the aspect statuses, evidence and explanation. Include positive findings and what to preserve. Explain Not applicable rather than penalizing a format for irrelevant features.

| Aspect | Status | Content evidence | Discovery reference where relevant | Why / remaining gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Evidence can be URL/section, file/paragraph, caption excerpt, slide number, thread entry, or transcript timestamp. Absence needs enough reviewed coverage to establish it. A long matrix is optional when prose communicates the same judgments clearly.

Expand material findings with observed text/behavior, pack/source status, consequence for the intended reader, a specific repair, and an observable completion check. Label suggested replacement copy as a proposal, preserve format/voice, and avoid unsupported or confidential additions. Separate confidence in the issue from uncertainty about AI outcomes.

## Remaining checks

List consequential Unknowns, the smallest evidence or discovery update that resolves them, and the decision unlocked. Draft-pack conflicts, independent fact verification, disclosure status, inaccessible sources, platform behavior, and performance data remain distinct. Conclude with scope limits; do not imply editorial changes establish AI citations or revenue.
