# Social post review

Use for captions, short professional posts, threads, carousels and media-supported posts. Record channel and unit: one entry, the whole thread, caption plus slides, or caption plus transcript. A LinkedIn article can also need the long-form review. Read only relevant modes.

## Review the actual unit

- **Hook and promise:** Does the opening accurately frame what the post offers? An engaging hook is not automatically misleading; identify the specific exaggeration or unmet promise.
- **One useful contribution:** Does the unit deliver an answer, concrete observation, example, trade-off, or useful perspective for its intended reader? A focused post need not contain an entire buyer guide.
- **Enough context:** Are the organization, product, category, use case, geography, time and limitations clear where they affect meaning? Do not insert the company name in every sentence. Existing audience familiarity can be relevant; state rather than assume it.
- **Cross-part coherence:** Compare claims in slides, caption, thread entries, video/audio transcript, and linked evidence when supplied. A caption caveat may not cure a prominent contradictory slide. Missing slides, inaccessible comments, cropped captions or unavailable audio stay Unknown.
- **Evidence and attribution:** Place meaningful qualifications with the claim and give a workable path to support where the format permits. A short claim can point to a maintained fact sheet; do not insist it reproduce a full research method. Clearly identify an owned case or paid/company relationship when material.
- **Useful next step:** A link, question, saveable explanation, or no CTA may all suit the purpose. Avoid boilerplate engagement bait or an automatic sales CTA on education content. Check the destination if it is part of the claim or user journey and accessible within scope. Separate observable CTA clarity from untested channel handling: a contact route not mentioned in the discovery pack is not a contradiction by itself. Keep its operation Unknown unless an actual defect or explicit restriction is established; do not downgrade clear wording merely because the route was not verified.
- **Voice and medium:** Preserve the author's voice and legitimate conversational style. For visual units assess legibility/context from actual supplied images. Do not judge unseen slides or assume a text transcript captures visual evidence.

## Avoid blog and platform assumptions

Short posts do not need H1/H2 tags, schema, FAQs, exhaustive comparisons, author bios, or a full keyword cluster. Do not mark these Missing merely because a blog can use them. An individual post's public accessibility does not establish indexing or AI use; a login wall or one failed fetch does not establish universal invisibility. Distinguish the inspected access path from unknown platform behavior.

Do not prescribe hashtag counts, frequency, external-link placement or format preference as algorithm rules without relevant current primary evidence. Brand self-publication may contribute useful information, but is not independent reputation evidence or proof of recommendation lift. Evaluate LinkedIn, Reddit, Instagram, and other channels according to the actual content and audience instead of transferring one study's source ranking to all posts.

When the post is necessarily brief, suggest one focused clarification or a supporting asset before suggesting a long rewrite. If the unit is adequate, say what to retain. A future source-strategy experiment is T3, not a correction needed for every post.
