## Strength of recommendations

- Repeated patterns motivate reviewing intent, source fit, identity, evidence, and mention order. They do not establish universal causal ranking factors.
- A vendor playbook proposes direct answers, comparisons, FAQs, dates, and links. Audit the information need they serve rather than requiring each format everywhere.
- Interviews explain embeddings, fan-out, language preferences, and proprietary retrieval stacks; those accounts are not independently verified implementations.
- Semantic domains, bilingual expansion, llms.txt, FAQ markup as a visibility lever, and robot-facing replicas lack controlled causal proof in this corpus. Treat them conditionally.
- Source importance differs by sector. Payment-terminal pages can be valuable owned sources for fees and specifications; external channels should follow demonstrated source roles.
- Podcast transcripts were generated automatically and proofread, not fully audio-verified. Inspect questionable terms or numbers before relying on them.

Separate recommendation provenance from website evidence. A Ranqia claim can motivate a hypothesis; it cannot establish that a target page lacks an aspect or that a change will improve AI outcomes.
